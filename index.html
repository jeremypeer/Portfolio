The Journey

From spreadsheets to strategy

Back in 2012, I was studying C++ and Visual Basic while watching companies like Amazon and Netflix use data to outpace everyone else. There wasn't a clear career path connecting programming and business yet — so I built my own, starting in accounting and steadily moving toward analytics.

Today I sit at that intersection: translating financial and operational data into dashboards that give leaders real-time clarity, instead of a monthly snapshot that's already outdated.

In 2023, I shared that career-change story with Business Insider — going from an Accountant to a Data Analyst without a traditional CS degree.
